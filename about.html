Hi, I'm Siyu Liu!

Here's a story about Siyu, a violin string, and a front-row seat. 🎻

I've always loved concerts, usually from somewhere in the back. One day, I got a front-row seat at a Juilliard recital. Mid-performance, the violinist's string snapped, but he kept playing! The entire audience held its breath until the final note, then burst into applause. For a moment, it felt like an invisible thread connected us all...

Turns out, I've been changing seats perspectives ever since.

That curiosity drew me to UX & HCI. Whenever I design an experience, I ask myself: whose seat haven't I tried sitting in yet?
