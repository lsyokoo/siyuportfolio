AGENTIC AI · MULTIMODAL UX

Multimodal AI Interactions with ByteDance

CHALLENGE

How might we transform AI-generated videos from passive viewing into interactive experiences?

SOLUTION

Combined agentic AI workflows with gesture and voice controls for real-time audiovisual interaction.

OUTCOME

Delivered an interactive AI experience to an audience of 60+ through live performances and screenings.

Role: Interaction Designer & Engineer
Timeline: Oct 2025 – Dec 2025
Tools: TouchDesigner · Max/MSP · Dreamina AI

Challenge

Beyond passive AI-generated content

In collaboration with ByteDance Dreamina AI, our team explored how AI-generated videos could become experiences people actively shape through movement, voice, and real-time feedback.

Agentic Exploration

From prompting to agentic creation

I experimented with prompt engineering to refine AI-generated visuals, then explored Dreamina's AI Agent for more complex, multi-step creation. I also evaluated the agent's usability and shared feedback with ByteDance's product team.

Multimodal Interaction

Giving participants control through gesture and voice

I designed and implemented gesture controls and voice input using TouchDesigner and Max/MSP, allowing participants to manipulate AI-generated visuals and contribute audio in real time.

Iterations

Making every interaction feel responsive

Across 9 rounds of prototype testing, I refined gesture recognition, interaction clarity, and audiovisual synchronization to create a more responsive and cohesive experience.

Impact

From interactive prototype to public experience

I worked with ByteDance PMs and international teams to align project timelines, coordinate AI Video Festival preparations, and refine the experience for public showcases.

Reflection

Designing beyond the screen

This project expanded how I think about interaction design, from evaluating agentic AI tools to designing real-time multimodal feedback. It reinforced the importance of testing interactions in practice and balancing technical constraints with user experience.
