Mosquito Havoc

Mosquito Havoc is a collaborative project built with Arduino and Unity where players flap their arms to fly as a mosquito trying to sting a baby. The project queers conventional interfaces by replacing efficiency with awkwardness and humor, turning physical struggle into empathy.
